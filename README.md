# 📊 Blinkit Sales & Customer Insights Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
The Blinkit Sales & Customer Insights Dashboard is a data analytics project designed to analyze and visualize business performance. It transforms raw sales data into meaningful insights to understand customer behavior, product trends, and overall business growth.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze sales performance across categories and locations  
- Identify top-performing products and outlets  
- Understand customer purchasing patterns  
- Enable data-driven decision-making  

---

## 📂 Dataset
The dataset includes:
- Product details (category, type, price)  
- Sales data (total sales, quantity sold)  
- Outlet information (location, size, type)  
- Customer behavior data  

---

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- 💰 Total Sales  
- 📦 Total Orders  
- 🛒 Average Sales per Order  
- ⭐ Top-Selling Products  
- 🏬 Sales by Outlet Type & Location  
- 📈 Category-wise Sales Distribution  

---

## 📈 Dashboard Insights
- Identifies high-performing product categories  
- Shows sales distribution across outlet types  
- Highlights customer purchasing trends  
- Detects growth opportunities and underperforming areas  

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
- Power BI  
- Excel / CSV  
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
